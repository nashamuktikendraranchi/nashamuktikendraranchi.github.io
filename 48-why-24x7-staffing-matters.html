When you are choosing a centre near Ranchi, 24x7 staffing is not a luxury phrase. It is a question about who is actually present, and able to act, when the day is at its most difficult.

What "24x7" should mean in practice

It should mean that a family can call, and that a resident is not left in an unsupervised corridor during the night. It should mean that a worsening tremor, confusion, chest pain, or a sudden collapse can be noticed and escalated without waiting for morning. It should mean that admission conversations can happen when a crisis has forced the issue, not only between tidy daytime slots. Ask who is on site after dark, how medical concerns are handled at night, and what happens if a hospital is needed.

A building that is locked, with no one clearly responsible, is not a place to go through alcohol withdrawal.

Who watches the first nights of detox? How does a resident reach staff after lights are dimmed?
